🍭 How to play

Basics

Swap and match: Drag, swipe or tap two neighbouring candies. Match at least three of the same colour. Invalid swaps are reversed.

Keyboard: Arrow keys move focus; Shift + arrow swaps. Enter or Space selects a candy. Esc pauses or resumes.

Combos: Falling candies can form more matches automatically, increasing your score multiplier.

⚡ Sugar Rush: Clear 40 candies to earn double points for 10 seconds in Timed or three valid moves in other modes.

Hint and Shuffle: Hint highlights a useful swap. Shuffle refreshes the board; you start with three shuffles.

Modes

⏱ Timed: Start with three minutes and earn 10 seconds per 100 points.

🎯 Adventure: Complete 100 levels with score, collection, clearance, special-candy, combo and obstacle missions. Each level has a move limit.

♾ Endless: No time or move limit. Press Finish to collect rewards and see your results.

📅 Daily: A date-based mission with a move limit. Complete it for coins and a streak.

🔥 Survival: Start with 15 moves and earn more through matches and bonuses. See the Survival tab for details.

Obstacles

Ice and jelly are cleared by matching or blasting their tiles. Locked candies and chocolate cannot be swapped; clear their tiles or neighbouring tiles. Chocolate can spread when a move clears none of it.

Candies

🍬 Match four in a line: Create a striped candy that clears a row or column when activated.

🌈 Match five or more in a line: Create a rainbow candy. Swap it with a candy to clear that colour.

💣 T/L match: Create a bomb that clears a 3×3 area when activated.

Special combinations

Striped + striped: clear a row and column. Striped + bomb: clear three rows and columns. Bomb + bomb: clear a 5×5 area.

Rainbow + striped or bomb: convert candies of the matching colour into specials and activate them. Rainbow + rainbow: clear the board.

Special candies can trigger other specials in a chain reaction.

Rewards can combine, but overlapping matches earn only their strongest match reward. Your balance cannot exceed 30 moves. A red warning appears at five or fewer moves.

One-time milestones

25 moves = 25 coins; 50 = 50; 100 = 100; 200 = 200; 500 = 300; 1,000 = 500. See your survival record and milestones in Menu → Profile.

Boosters

Open Boosters to use your inventory. Buy more with Sweet Coins in Menu → Shop.

🔨 Hammer: Clear a selected candy.

💣 Mega bomb: Clear a 5×5 area around a selected candy.

🌈 Colour blast: Clear candies of the selected colour.

🔀 Super shuffle: Refresh the board.

➕ Extra moves: Add five moves in Adventure, Daily or Survival (up to the 30-move cap).

⏱ Extra time: Add 30 seconds in Timed.

👆 Free swap: Allow the next swap without a match.

⚡ Sugar Rush: Activate double points.

One undo per game is available through the Boosters menu before another booster or purchase; rewards and statistics from the undone move are reversed. Targeted boosters are only consumed when used. Press Escape or use Cancel selected booster in Boosters to cancel.

Rewards

🪙 Sweet Coins: Earn rewards from Adventure stars, daily challenges, achievements and Survival milestones. Claim 50 daily login coins in Menu → Home. Tap the coin counter to open the shop.

Gameplay rewards: Every 100 points earns 5 coins; create a striped, bomb or rainbow candy for 1, 2 or 3 coins respectively. Combo ×5 earns 5, ×10 earns 15, and naturally triggered Sugar Rush earns 5. Beat an existing high score and finish the game for 25 coins.

Completion: Timed, Endless and Survival earn up to 30 coins at completion (one per 30 points). First completion of each tenth Adventure stage earns 100 extra coins. First 7-day and 30-day daily streak milestones earn 250 and 1,000 coins.

⭐ Stars: Finish an Adventure mission within 55% of its original move limit for three stars, or 80% for two; otherwise earn one. Improved stars award 30 coins each.

📅 Daily: First completion of a daily challenge awards 100 coins. Consecutive completions build your streak.

🏆 Achievements: Unlock trophies and 50 coins per achievement. View them in Menu → Profile.

🎨 Themes: Unlock alternative themes for 200 coins each.

Settings: Adjust music, sound, voice and vibration; switch tracks, use fullscreen or export/import progress. Use Menu → Home to resume saved gameplay.
